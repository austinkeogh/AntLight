Antlight Support

Antlight is a turn-based maze game for iPhone. Lead your ant through thirty dark mazes, on Earth, on the moon and in Fantasy Land, collect five treasures in each and reach the goal without being caught by a scorpion.

How to play

  • Tap the arrows to move, or ✧ to wait a turn.
  • You can only see the tiles near your ant. Collect five treasures, then reach the goal.
  • Every three moves, each scorpion that has seen your ant in the last six moves steps towards it. Any other scorpion waits where it is. If one catches your ant, tap ↻ to try the level again.
  • Tap 🏠 to open the level menu and replay any level you've unlocked, or ? to see how to play.
  • Finish a level in fewer moves to earn up to three stars.

Common questions

Is my progress saved? Yes. Each level you finish, and your best star rating for it, is saved on your iPhone straight away. A level you leave part-way through starts again from the beginning.

How do I earn three stars? Use fewer moves. Three stars need a finish within about a quarter of the shortest possible route. Waiting a turn counts as a move; bumping into an obstacle doesn't. Replay any level from the 🏠 menu to improve your rating.

How do I start again from level 1? To replay level 1, choose it from the 🏠 menu. To lock the later levels again, delete Antlight and reinstall it, which erases your saved progress.

There's no sound. Antlight follows Silent Mode. Turn Silent Mode off and turn the volume up.

I don't feel any vibrations. Check that Settings > Sounds & Haptics > System Haptics is on.
