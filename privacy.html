Antlight Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

Antlight is a game for iPhone made by Diarmuid Keogh. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

What Antlight collects

Nothing. Antlight doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet.

What stays on your device

To remember how far you've got, Antlight saves your progress on your device: the level you're on, the highest level you've unlocked and your best star rating on each level. It never leaves your device, except as part of the backups you make yourself, such as iCloud Backup. Deleting the app deletes it.

Antlight plays sounds and uses your device's haptics. It doesn't use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Reports from Apple

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send me crash reports and other diagnostic information about Antlight. Apple controls what these reports contain, and you can turn sharing off at any time under Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Antlight doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
